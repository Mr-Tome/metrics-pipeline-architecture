Full architecture

Metrics are intended to be used to evaluate on Army Systems: the current system targets are SUTX (BPG2)

Data flows left to right through the following stages: "Detected and Registered", "Identified and Standardized" into one common model, "Scored" by the metric library, "Presented", and "Published" for exploration.

The analyst workbench lets the joint analysis team explore in Jupyter and turn what they find into tested metric plugins. AI will enable drafting, testing, and production of additional plugins and evals.

The AI maintenance loop has multiple purposes: schema / IDL change detection, plugin & eval updates, test changes on datasets, then creating MRs for human reviewers.

The tech stack view shows the proposed tool for each part. The deployment view shows what this looks like running on the lab's Kubernetes cluster.
